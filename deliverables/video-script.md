# VerdictProof — GenLayer Projects Demo

Target duration: 3 minutes 38 seconds

Narration voice: local Windows Speech Synthesis (`Microsoft David Desktop`). No ElevenLabs or external text-to-speech API is used.

## Narration

Campaigns is the public evidence marketplace. A sponsor defines a real product task, proof requirements, a minimum score, tester stake, and a funded GEN reward pool. A tester completes the task and submits a finalized Bradbury transaction, a public outcome URL, and written feedback.

Review is the command center for evidence awaiting judgment. It shows the selected campaign, submitted proofs, and the five-stage validation lifecycle: read the brief, inspect proof URLs, analyze feedback, reach a consensus score, then reward or slash. This pilot currently shows zero pending reviews because all three submissions have already reached finalized decisions.

Dashboard is the permanent audit surface, separate from the pending Review queue. GenLayer verifies objective receipt facts, then uses independent comparative validation for semantic judgments that deterministic code cannot make. Every reviewed submission on this page links to a full GenLayer consensus report read from finalized contract state.

This approved report shows the score, campaign threshold, settlement, original task, proof requirement, evidence URLs, and tester wallet. The three hard gates expose transaction execution, wallet identity, and task completion. Below them, the report shows all four rubric components: proof, feedback, insight, and originality, with the consensus-committed rationale for each score.

The frontend does not invent validator analysis. Narrative fields come from the leader report committed only after independent validators agree on the material evidence gates, decision side, and bounded score tolerances.

The consensus section verifies the exact review transaction against Bradbury RPC. It displays the real lifecycle, execution result, method, contract, and recorded votes. This approved review finalized with AGREE and finished successfully. Three of five validators agreed; timeout votes remain visible instead of being rewritten.

VerdictProof also demonstrates two real rejection paths. One submission fails the objective identity gate because the receipt sender does not match the tester wallet. Another passes receipt and identity checks but is rejected because the rendered outcome does not prove the requested task.

Claims is the wallet-specific payout workspace for approved stake and rewards. The public campaign history confirms that the approved tester already claimed the reserved stake and reward. After every submission was settled, the sponsor closed the campaign and withdrew the remaining pool. VerdictProof therefore completes the full Bradbury workflow: campaigns, review, finalized dashboard reports, claims, and refund settlement.

## Recording rules

- Record 1920×1080 at 30 FPS.
- Keep the cursor visible and move it deliberately.
- Use short pauses before every click and after every navigation.
- Scroll in small increments; never jump to anchors while narration is explaining a section.
- Do not connect a wallet or create new transactions during the recording.
- Do not claim five-of-five agreement for review transactions; the verified review reports record three-of-five agreement.
